Who We Are

Sanima GIC Insurance Ltd.

Sanima GIC Insurance Ltd. is a premier non-life insurance company formed through the strategic consolidation of Sanima General Insurance Ltd. and General Insurance Company Nepal Ltd.

The merger was officially completed on October 24, 2022, creating a stronger, more resilient insurance institution registered under Registration No. 1001/63/64 with the Office of the Company Registrar, Government of Nepal.

"Over 5 years of excellent service in the general insurance sector, continuing with enhanced capability and wider reach post-merger."

Our Journey

Company History

From two independent entities to a unified insurance powerhouse.

Pre-2018
Dual Establishment
Sanima General Insurance Ltd. and General Insurance Company Nepal Ltd. both established and operated independently in Nepal's growing insurance sector.
2018–2022
Growth & Expansion
Both companies grew their branch networks across Nepal's 7 provinces, building strong customer bases and reinsurance partnerships with global leaders.
Oct 24, 2022
Landmark Merger
The two companies officially merged to form Sanima GIC Insurance Ltd., creating a stronger entity with 400+ staff, 50+ branches, and greater financial capacity.
2023–Present
Unified Excellence
Sanima GIC continues to grow post-merger, expanding its product portfolio, digital services, and nationwide presence under a unified brand and leadership.
