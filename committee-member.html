Committee Governance Structure

Our committees comprise experienced professionals who oversee governance, risk management, audit functions, and regulatory compliance. Each committee plays a vital role in ensuring organizational excellence and building stakeholder confidence in Sanima GIC Insurance.

Governance

Board Committees

Audit Committee
Ensures financial accuracy, transparency and regulatory compliance in all operations
Risk Management Committee
Oversees identification, assessment and mitigation of enterprise-wide risks
Investment Committee
Guides capital allocation and investment strategies for optimal returns
Remuneration Committee
Determines fair and competitive compensation structures for leadership
Grievance Committee
Addresses policyholder concerns ensuring timely and fair resolution
Corporate Governance Committee
Upholds ethical standards and governance best practices across the organization
